Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with TikTok, Openmetri receives an access token and basic profile information (display name and avatar) for the account you authorize. It also stores the videos, titles, captions and schedule you create in the app, and the publishing status of each post.

How we use it

The data is used only to publish your own videos to your own TikTok account, to show you the status of your posts and to let you manage your schedule. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored locally on the owner's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server other than TikTok when you publish a post.

Retention and deletion

Access tokens and post data stay only as long as the account is connected. You can disconnect the account in the app, and you can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and services). On request, all stored data is deleted.

Children

Openmetri is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: github.com/KIQ232/openmetri/issues.
