Openmetri

Openmetri is a personal social media management tool. It lets the owner of a TikTok account schedule and publish their own videos (and review the status of those posts) from one place, instead of uploading each video by hand on the phone.

How it works with TikTok

The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app. Openmetri then sends the owner's own videos, with the title and caption the owner wrote, to that same TikTok account through the Content Posting API. Only the account owner uses the app, and it only acts on the account that authorized it.
